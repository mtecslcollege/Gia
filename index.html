About GIA

A school where every child is known, guided and inspired

Based in Makama, Makeni, GIA brings the whole journey of learning under one roof. Our motto, Seat of Wisdom, guides everything we do: sound teaching, good character and a safe, orderly school community that parents can trust.

  • A complete pathway from Day-care to S.S.S 3
  • Clear leadership, from the Proprietor to the Form-masters
  • Science, Art and Commercial departments, each with a Head
  • Online registration and a secure School Portal for families
