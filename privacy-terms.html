Part 1 · Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Gbonkolenken International Academy ("GIA", "we", "us") is a school at New Jersey - Makama - Makeni, Sierra Leone. This policy covers our public website, online registration form and School Portal (our school management system).

2. Information we collect

  • Admission details: student and parent or guardian names, date of birth, class applied for, address, phone number, email and previous school.
  • School records: attendance, assessments, results, fee records and welfare or discipline notes, kept in the School Portal.
  • Staff details: names, roles, qualifications and contact information.
  • Website data: basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working.

3. How we use information

  • To process admissions and place learners in the right class.
  • To teach, assess, report on progress and keep students safe.
  • To manage fees, notices and communication with parents and guardians.
  • To run the school, meet legal duties and improve our services.
We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Children's information

Most of the information we hold is about children, so we collect only what the school needs and limit access to authorised staff. Photographs of students on our website or in the gallery are used to show school life. Parents or guardians may ask us to remove a photograph of their child at any time.

5. Sharing, security and retention

We share information only with people who need it to educate or protect a child, with education authorities where required, or when the law demands it. School Portal access is protected by individual accounts and passwords, and records are kept only as long as needed for school and legal purposes.

6. Your rights

Parents, guardians and staff may ask to see, correct or request deletion of their information, subject to school and legal record-keeping requirements. Please contact the school office using the details below.

Part 2 · Terms of Use

7. Using our website

By using this website you agree to use it lawfully and respectfully. You must not try to damage the site, gain unauthorised access, upload harmful files or misuse other people's information.

8. School Portal accounts

  • Accounts are issued by the school to parents, students and staff for their own use only.
  • Keep your password private and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.
  • We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or puts the safety of school records at risk.

9. Online registration and fees

Sending a registration form is an application, not a guarantee of admission. Admission, class placement and fees are confirmed by the school after review. Please give true and complete information; false details may lead to an offer being withdrawn. Admission is open only while the notice on the Academic Programmes page says so.

10. Content and liability

The GIA name, crest, text and photographs belong to the school, or are used with permission, and may not be copied for commercial use without written consent. We work to keep information accurate and the site available, but we are not liable for interruptions or for decisions made only on website content. Please confirm important details with the school office.

11. Changes and governing law

We may update this page from time to time; the date above shows the latest version. These terms are governed by the laws of Sierra Leone.
