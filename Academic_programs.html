How We Teach

How every course is taught and assessed

Every programme joins classroom teaching with practical learning. Students are assessed through classwork, tests, practical work, projects and end-of-term examinations.

  1. Classroom lessons led by qualified teachers
  2. Practical laboratory lessons in Science
  3. Demonstrations and guided practice
  4. Group work, projects and assignments
  5. Reading time and library use
  6. Continuous assessment and feedback
  7. Guidance and care from Form-masters
  8. End-of-term examinations and BECE or WASSCE preparation
