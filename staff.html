A Guide for Parents

Who should I speak to?

Start with the person closest to your child's question. If it cannot be solved there, move up one step.

Where to take your question
Your questionSpeak to
Everyday class matters, attendance or behaviourYour child's Form-master
A concern about a whole stage (Day-care, Kindergarten, Grade, J.S.S, S.S.S)The Coordinator for that stage
A subject, practical lesson or examination questionThe Subject Teacher, then the Head of Department
Admission, enrolment and school recordsThe Senior Administrative Officer
School fees, payments and receiptsThe Finance Officer
Discipline and daily school operationsThe Vice-Principal
Serious concerns, school policy or complaintsThe Principal
