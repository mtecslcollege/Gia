Who We Are

A school with a public purpose

Gbonkolenken International Academy is a private school with a public purpose. We were founded on a simple belief: every child deserves an education that truly prepares them for life.

We encourage understanding instead of learning by rote. Our students are taught to think carefully, behave with discipline and dream without limits. From Day-care through Senior Secondary School, we raise young men and women who are not only good at examinations, but also responsible, thoughtful and ready to lead.

School History

Bridging the school gap in Sierra Leone

  1. 3 September 2023

    Our founding

    The school was founded as a private institution to improve how children learn in Sierra Leone. From the beginning, our goal has been a new standard of education built on discipline, careful thinking and real-life learning.

  2. Our vision

    A clear direction

    To become a leading school in Makeni and a model for Sierra Leone, known for academic excellence and discipline, and for graduates who lead with integrity and good character.

  3. Growth

    Steady progress

    The school keeps growing its classes, improving its classrooms and laboratories, and training its teachers and young leaders.

  4. Today

    A growing school family

    We exist to give students an education that goes beyond textbooks, so that they succeed in further study, in their careers and in leadership, in Sierra Leone and beyond.

School Leadership

How the school is organised

Every learner is supported by a clear chain of responsibility. Meet our staff →

  • ProprietorHolds overall ownership and sets the long-term vision and policy of the school.
  • PrincipalChief administrator, responsible for daily academic leadership and school management.
  • Vice-PrincipalAssists the Principal with academic supervision, student discipline and daily operations.
  • Senior Administrative OfficerOversees administrative services, school records and daily office operations.
  • Finance OfficerManages school fees, receipts and financial reports.
  • CoordinatorsOne for each stage: Day-care, Kindergarten, Grade, Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary. They oversee daily learning and student welfare.
  • Heads of Department (H.O.D’s)H.O.D Science, H.O.D Art and H.O.D Commercial lead their departments and supervise subject teachers.
  • Form-mastersThe first contact for a class: they monitor attendance, conduct and welfare.
  • Subject TeachersTeach, assess and guide students in each subject.
  • School PrefectRepresents students and supports leadership and welfare, working with school management.
  • General StudentsThe whole student body, from Day-care to Senior Secondary, whom the school exists to serve.
